In the [sandbox](/guides/sandbox), checkout runs on the payment provider's test mode. No money moves and nothing you do touches production. The details on this page make a sandbox checkout behave a certain way: a successful payment, a decline, a 3D Secure prompt, a dispute, or a wallet, SEPA Direct Debit or UPI payment.

## Before you start

* **Any test card takes any future expiry date and any CVC.** Use 3 digits for the CVC, or 4 for American Express. Any valid postal code works.
* **Never enter a real card number in the sandbox.** Test mode rejects real cards.
* **Checkout shows the methods that fit the customer's country and the checkout currency.** To test UPI or SEPA Direct Debit, give the plan an INR or EUR price with [location-based pricing](/product-catalog/location-based-pricing) and choose the matching billing country at checkout. See [Payment methods](/checkout/payment-methods) for which method needs which currency.
* **Redirect methods open a test page instead of the wallet or bank.** The page has **Authorize test payment** and **Fail test payment** buttons. Choose one to finish the payment.
* **SEPA Direct Debit finishes in about 3 minutes in the sandbox.** It takes several business days in production. See [Timing in the sandbox](#timing-in-the-sandbox).

After each test, check the order, customer, subscription and entitlements in the sandbox dashboard, and the [webhook events](/guides/webhooks) your endpoint received.

### Invalid card details

These errors come from the checkout form itself, before anything is sent to the payment provider.

| Scenario | What to enter |
| --- | --- |
| Invalid expiry month | Month `13` |
| Invalid expiry year | A year in the past, such as `95` |
| Invalid CVC | A 2-digit CVC, such as `99` |
| Invalid number | A number that fails the Luhn check, such as `4242 4242 4242 4241` |

## Subscriptions and saved cards

A subscription charges the saved card again at each renewal without the customer present. These cards behave differently on the first payment and on renewals.

| Card number | At checkout | On renewal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `4000 0025 0000 3155` | 3D Secure prompt | Succeeds, because the card was set up at checkout |
| `4000 0027 6000 3184` | 3D Secure prompt | Renewal needs authentication, so the automatic charge fails |
| `4000 0038 0000 0446` | 3D Secure prompt | Succeeds |
| `4000 0082 6000 3178` | 3D Secure prompt, then declined for insufficient funds | Declined for insufficient funds |
| `4000 0000 0000 0341` | Saved successfully. A paid plan is declined right away; a free trial starts | Every charge fails |

To run a renewal without waiting a month, use the Daily billing cycle described in [Test a subscription renewal](/guides/sandbox#test-a-subscription-renewal). Failed renewals follow the retry schedule in [Failed subscription payments](/product-catalog/subscriptions#failed-subscription-payments).

## Wallets

### Apple Pay and Google Pay

Apple Pay and Google Pay have no test cards. In the sandbox, pay with a real card in your wallet: test mode recognises it, returns a test token and never charges the card. Test cards cannot be added to a wallet.

* **Apple Pay** needs Safari on a device with a card in Apple Wallet.
* **Google Pay** needs a browser signed in to a Google account with at least one real card saved. Once the Google Pay sheet opens, you can also pick a card from Google's test card suite.

If the wallet button does not appear, the device or browser does not meet the wallet's requirements.

### Link

Link saves card details for one-click checkout. In the sandbox, create a Link account with any email address. The one-time passcode decides the outcome.

| Passcode | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Any 6 digits not listed below | Signs in |
| `000001` | Code invalid |
| `000002` | Code expired |
| `000003` | Too many attempts |

Do not store real personal data in sandbox Link accounts.

### Amazon Pay, Kakao Pay and Naver Pay

Select the wallet and click **Pay**. The test page opens. Click **Authorize test payment** to succeed or **Fail test payment** to fail. There are no special test values.

Kakao Pay and Naver Pay need a KRW price and a customer in South Korea.

## UPI

Select UPI and click **Pay**. A QR code appears. Scan it with your phone to open a UPI test payment page, where you can **Authorize** or **Expire** the payment. There are no test UPI IDs: checkout only takes UPI by QR code.

The same flow sets up the UPI AutoPay mandate for a subscription. Limits, AutoPay and refunds are on the [UPI page](/checkout/payment-methods/upi).

## Timing in the sandbox

| Method | Sandbox | Production |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Link | Immediate | Immediate |
| Amazon Pay, Kakao Pay, Naver Pay | Immediate, after the test page | Immediate, after the wallet |
| SEPA Direct Debit | Immediate, or about 3 minutes for the delayed IBANs | Several business days |
| UPI | Immediate, after the test page | Immediate, after the customer pays |
| Refunds | Immediate, except the two asynchronous refund cards | Minutes to several days |
