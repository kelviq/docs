A checkout session is created each time a customer opens a checkout link or your app creates a checkout through the API. [Orders](/sales/orders) only show purchases that went through. Checkout sessions show every attempt, so you can see where customers stop.

Open **Sales → Checkout sessions**.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/checkout-sessions/checkout-sessions-list.webp" alt="The Checkout sessions page with the status filter open, listing sessions with their amount, status, customer, product, and date" />
</Frame>

Each row shows the session ID, the amount and billing period, the status, the customer's country and email (or N/A if they never entered one), the product and plan, and when the session was created. Search by name or session ID, filter by status, and change the date range above the list.

## Session statuses

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Open** | The customer has the checkout and hasn't paid yet. |
| **Processing** | The customer submitted a payment and it's being confirmed. |
| **Success** | The payment went through and an [order](/sales/orders) was created. |
| **Closed** | The session ended without a payment before it expired. |
| **Expired** | Nobody paid within four days of the session being created. |

A session that's still **Open** or **Processing** an hour after it was created, with no customer activity in the last 30 minutes, is treated as abandoned and gets [recovery emails](/sales/abandoned-cart-recovery) if the customer entered an email address.

## Session details

Click a session to open its details: the items in the checkout, the customer, the payment method used, and the session timeline.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/checkout-sessions/checkout-session-detail.webp" alt="Details of an expired checkout session, showing the purchased item and a timeline from Checkout started to Checkout session expired" />
</Frame>

The timeline lists what happened in the session, newest first, with the time of each event. For example:

| Event | When it's added |
| --- | --- |
| **Checkout started** | The customer opened the checkout. Shows whether it's a subscription or one-time purchase, and the amount. |
| **Plan selected** | The customer picked a plan or billing period. Shows the plan, period, and price. |
| **Billing address updated** | The customer entered or changed their country or address. |
| **Checkout session abandoned** | The customer left without paying. |
| **Checkout session expired** | The session reached its four-day limit without a payment. |

Payment attempts appear in the timeline too, including the decline reason when a payment fails, such as `insufficient_funds`. Use it to answer "why didn't this customer get charged?" without asking them to try again.

## Custom field responses

If the checkout collected [custom fields](/checkout/custom-fields), the customer's answers are saved on the session as `customFieldsData` in the API, and sent as `custom_fields_data` in the `checkout.completed` [webhook](/guides/webhooks#custom-field-responses).
