Connect [Mailchimp](https://mailchimp.com) to keep each Kelviq customer in a Mailchimp audience, tagged with their plan and status: active, on a trial, cancelled, with a failed payment, and more. Use the tags to send campaigns and Customer Journeys to exactly the right people, for example a win-back email to cancelled customers or a tip email to everyone on Pro. Kelviq verifies every event before it changes anything.

It works with Mailchimp's free plan.

## What Kelviq does

For each event, Kelviq adds the customer to your audience if they aren't there yet, with their first and last name, and then sets their tags:

| Tag | Added when | Removed when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `kelviq-customer` | Any event about the customer | Never |
| `kelviq-active` | A subscription starts, resumes or changes | It's paused or cancelled |
| `kelviq-plan-<plan>`, like `kelviq-plan-pro` | A subscription is on that plan | The plan changes, or it's cancelled |
| `kelviq-trial` | A subscription is trialing | The trial ends |
| `kelviq-trial-ending` | A trial is about to end | Never |
| `kelviq-paused` | A subscription is paused | It resumes |
| `kelviq-cancelled` | A subscription is cancelled | A new subscription starts |
| `kelviq-payment-failed` | An invoice payment fails | An invoice is paid |
| `kelviq-purchased` | A checkout completes | Never |
| `kelviq-refunded` | A refund is made | Never |
| `kelviq-usage-alert` | A feature usage alert | Never |

Your own tags on the same people are never changed.

### Email consent

You choose how **new** people join your audience:

- **Transactional** (default): they're in your audience and tagged, but don't get marketing emails. Use this unless they agreed to marketing.
- **Subscribed**: they get your marketing emails. Only use this if your checkout asks for marketing consent.
- **Pending**: Mailchimp emails them to confirm (double opt-in).

People already in your audience keep their status. If someone unsubscribed, Kelviq never subscribes them again.

## Before you start

1. **Create an API key.** In Mailchimp, click your profile picture → **Profile → Extras → API keys → Create A Key**. Name it `Kelviq` and copy it. It ends with your data center, like `-us21`.
2. **Find your Audience ID.** Open **Audience → All contacts → Settings → Audience name and defaults**, and copy the **Audience ID**, like `a1b2c3d4e5`.

<Note>
  Mailchimp's free plan includes up to 250 contacts. Kelviq customers count toward that limit, also when they're transactional.
</Note>

## Connect Mailchimp

1. Open **Settings → Integrations**.
2. On the **Mailchimp** card, click **Configure Mailchimp**.
3. Paste the **API key** and the **Audience ID**.
4. Choose how to **add new people**. Keep **Transactional** unless you have marketing consent.
5. Optionally, enter a **Name**, such as `Customers audience`. It's only used to label the connection in Kelviq.
6. Choose the **Events to send**. Customer, checkout, subscription, invoice and refund events are selected.
7. Click **Connect Mailchimp**.

Kelviq checks the key and the audience without adding anyone. From then on, customers are added and tagged on each selected event.

<Note>
  Connections are separate for sandbox and live. Use a separate Mailchimp audience for sandbox, so test customers stay out of your real audience.
</Note>

## Manage a connection

Open **Settings → Integrations → Configure Mailchimp** to see your connected audiences. For each one you can:

- **Send test**: check the key and audience still work. Nobody is added.
- **Edit events**: change which events are sent, then click **Save events**.
- **Disconnect**: stop updating Mailchimp. People and tags already there stay.

Each connection is a Kelviq webhook endpoint, so it also appears under **Settings → Webhooks**, named `Mailchimp: <name>`. There you can see every delivery and resend one that failed. Your API key is stored encrypted and is never shown.

## Troubleshooting

The delivery's response in **Settings → Webhooks** says what happened:

| Response | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Mailchimp did not accept this API key | The key was deleted or mistyped. Create a new key, then disconnect and connect again. |
| Mailchimp can't find this audience | Check the Audience ID. It must belong to the same Mailchimp account as the key. |
| This person unsubscribed or was removed from Mailchimp | Nothing to do. Kelviq respects unsubscribes and leaves them out. |
| Mailchimp did not accept this email address | Mailchimp rejects addresses that look fake. Nothing to do. |
| … has no customer email to add to Mailchimp | The Kelviq customer has no email. |

If Mailchimp is briefly unavailable or rate limits your account, Kelviq retries the delivery automatically.
