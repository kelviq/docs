Open **Settings → Profile** to change your own details and your organization's name.

## Your details

Change your **first name** and **last name** here. Your **primary email address** is the one you sign in with and can't be edited on this page.

If you signed up with Google, you sign in with Google by default. Click **Set password** to add a password so you can also sign in with your email address.

## Time preferences

* **Timezone:** the timezone the dashboard uses to show dates and times, such as when an order was paid.
* **Start week on:** the first day of the week in date ranges and calendars.

These change how dates are shown in the dashboard. They don't change billing dates, renewals, or anything customers see.

## Organization

**Organization name** is the name of your Kelviq organization. To change what customers see on checkout, such as your logo and colors, use [Checkout design](/checkout/checkout-design).
