title: 'Organizations'
description: 'Give each product or brand its own organization, and switch between them from the sidebar'
---

An organization holds one business in Kelviq: its products, customers, API keys and settings. When you launch a second product or brand, create a new organization for it instead of mixing both in one catalog.

## Create an organization

1. Open **Settings → Organizations** and click **Create organization**. The same button is in the organization switcher in the sidebar.
2. Enter the **Organization name**, for example "Acme Mobile". Customers see this name on checkout, receipts and emails.
3. Click **Create**.

Kelviq opens the new organization right away. It starts empty, so add its products, plans and checkout design the way you did for your first one.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Who can create** | The owner of an organization. Members who aren't owners are asked to contact the owner. A member who owns an organization of their own can create one, and it's created under the organization they own. |
| **Where** | Live mode only. In sandbox you can see the list but not create. |
| **Name** | Up to 200 characters. |

## Switch between organizations

Pick an organization in the switcher at the top of the sidebar, or click **Switch** next to it in **Settings → Organizations**. The one you're in is marked **Current**.

The list shows your organization first, then every organization created under it.

## What a new organization shares

Products, customers, API keys and settings are separate. Two things carry over from the organization it was created under:

| | How it works |
| --- | --- |
| **Payout account** | Add the new organization's business details. Once they're approved, open its **Payouts** page and click **Use [parent]'s payout account**. Its payouts then go to the same bank account. |
| **Owner** | **Settings → Members** shows the parent organization as the owner, as "[parent] (owner)". That row can't be edited or removed. |
