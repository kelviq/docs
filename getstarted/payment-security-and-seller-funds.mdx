Kelviq is the Merchant of Record, and every payment is processed by Stripe. Your customers' card details stay with Stripe, and your revenue goes to the payout account you set up in Kelviq.

## Card details stay with Stripe

Kelviq Checkout uses [Stripe Elements](https://docs.stripe.com/payments/elements) for the payment fields. Stripe secures the card number and CVC in the customer's browser, so they never reach Kelviq's servers. Kelviq receives the payment result only.

When a card issuer asks for it, Stripe runs 3D Secure to confirm the cardholder, which also helps prevent fraud.

## Disputes and chargebacks are handled for you

As the Merchant of Record, Kelviq responds to disputes, chargebacks, and payment reviews from banks and card networks.

## How you get paid

Customers pay Kelviq. Kelviq sends your share, after tax and fees, to your payout account, and you withdraw it to your bank. Track every step in the **Payouts** dashboard. See [Payouts](/payouts/overview).
