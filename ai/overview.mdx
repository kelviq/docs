Kelviq works with the AI tools you already use. Your coding agent can create your plans, add checkout and feature checks to your app, and wire up webhooks. You review and approve each change.

## For humans

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Set up with AI" icon="wand-magic-sparkles" href="/guides/set-up-with-ai">
    Copy a prompt from the dashboard and paste it into Claude Code, Cursor, Codex, Lovable, Replit, or Bolt.
  </Card>
  <Card title="CLI" icon="terminal" href="/cli/overview">
    Define products, features, and plans as code and sync them from the terminal.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

The quickest start:

1. Open **Settings → Set up with AI** in the dashboard.
2. Copy the keys block into your project's `.env.local`.
3. Copy the prompt and paste it into your agent as the first message.

Start in sandbox, where your agent builds and tests with test cards. When it works, `kelviq promote` copies your catalog to production. Business verification and your keys stay with you.
