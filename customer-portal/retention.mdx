---
title: "Retention offers"
description: "Ask customers why they're cancelling and make an offer to keep them"
---

When a customer cancels a subscription in the [customer portal](/customer-portal/overview), Kelviq asks why, then shows the offer you picked for that reason: a discount, a pause, a cheaper plan, or a link to your support team. If the customer says no, they continue to cancel.

Set it up in **Settings → Retention** and turn on **Enable retention offer**.

## Set up your offers

Set each offer up once. Then choose which reasons show it.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/retention/retention-offers.webp" alt="Retention settings with the Discount, Pause, Plan change, and Support offers" />
</Frame>

| Offer | What you set | What happens if the customer accepts |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Discount | A percentage off, for 1, 2, or 3 billing cycles | The plan stays the same and the next payments are discounted. After that, the price goes back to normal |
| Pause | Which lengths to offer: 1, 3, or 6 months | Billing stops for the period they choose, then resumes |
| Plan change | The next cheaper plan | They move to that plan. Customers already on your cheapest plan don't see this offer |
| Support | A link to a booking page, live chat, or your help site | They go to that link instead of cancelling |

Use the eye icon next to an offer to preview it.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/retention/retention-discount-offer.webp" alt="A discount offer of 20% off the next 3 payments" />
</Frame>

## Match offers to reasons

Under **Offers by cancellation reason**, pick an offer for each reason, or **No offer** to let the customer go straight to the final step.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/retention/retention-offers-by-reason.webp" alt="Offers by cancellation reason, with an offer chosen for each reason" />
</Frame>

| Reason | Example offer |
| --- | --- |
| Too expensive | Discount |
| Not using it enough | Pause |
| Missing features | Support |
| Switching to another product | Plan change |
| Technical issues | Support |
| Poor support experience | Support |
| Business or project ended | No offer |
| Other | No offer |

<Tip>
  Offer what fits the reason. A customer who isn't using the product won't stay for a discount, but might come back after a pause.
</Tip>

## Ask for written feedback

Turn on **Written feedback** to ask one open question before the customer confirms, such as "What could we do better?". Answering is optional.

## Advanced settings

| Setting | Options |
| --- | --- |
| Resume billing behavior | **Reset billing cycle** charges a full period when a paused subscription resumes and moves the billing date to that day. **Keep billing day** keeps the original billing date |
| Offer repeat policy | **Once per subscription**, **After previous offer ends**, or **Unlimited**. Controls whether a customer can accept an offer again on a later cancellation |
