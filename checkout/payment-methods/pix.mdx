Pix is Brazil's instant payment system, run by the Banco Central do Brasil. Your customer pays from their bank app and the payment goes through in seconds.

You don't sign up with a Pix provider or a Brazilian bank. Kelviq is the merchant of record, so Pix shows up at checkout for the right customers and the money reaches you in your normal [payout](/payouts/overview).

## Who sees Pix at checkout

| Condition | Why |
| --- | --- |
| The customer is in Brazil | Pix is a Brazilian payment system. |
| The checkout is priced in BRL | Pix only works in reais. |

Add a localized BRL price to your plan with [Price localization](/product-catalog/location-based-pricing). Customers in Brazil then see the BRL price, and Pix next to cards.

## Subscriptions with Pix Automático

Pix Automático is Pix's recurring payment option. The customer authorizes it once in their bank app at checkout, and Kelviq charges each renewal automatically, without the customer approving every payment.

| | One-time Pix | Pix Automático renewal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Customer action | Pay each time | Authorize once at checkout |
| Good for | One-time purchases | Subscriptions |

## Refunds

Refund a Pix payment from the order in [Orders](/sales/orders) with **Refund payment**. As with every refund on Kelviq, the transaction fee isn't returned and the tax part goes back to the customer.

## Pix questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need to sign up with a Pix provider?">
    No. Kelviq is the merchant of record and collects Pix payments for you. You only need a BRL price on your plan.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is Pix Automático the same as a scheduled Pix?">
    No. With Pix Automático, Kelviq requests each renewal under the customer's one-time authorization. A scheduled Pix is set up by the customer in their own bank app.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I also offer Boleto in Brazil?">
    Yes. Boleto is also available for customers in Brazil, and each renewal gets a new voucher. See [payment methods](/checkout/payment-methods#vouchers).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
