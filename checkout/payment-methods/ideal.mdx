iDEAL is a Dutch online payment method. Your customer pays straight from their Dutch bank account through their own banking app or online banking. iDEAL now runs under the name iDEAL | Wero.

You don't sign up with iDEAL or a Dutch bank. Kelviq is the merchant of record, so iDEAL shows up at checkout for the right customers and the money reaches you in your normal [payout](/payouts/overview).

## Who sees iDEAL at checkout

| Condition | Why |
| --- | --- |
| The customer is in the Netherlands | iDEAL works with Dutch banks. |
| The checkout is priced in EUR | iDEAL only works in euros. |

If your plans are priced in USD, add a localized EUR price with [Price localization](/product-catalog/location-based-pricing) so customers in the Netherlands see iDEAL.

## How the customer pays

iDEAL is a bank redirect. The customer picks iDEAL at checkout, is sent to their bank or banking app to approve the payment, then comes back to checkout.

## Subscriptions with iDEAL

The first payment is made with iDEAL. Renewals are then charged by SEPA Direct Debit, so the customer doesn't approve each renewal.

| | First payment | Renewals |
| --- | --- | --- |
| How it's paid | iDEAL, approved in the bank app | SEPA Direct Debit |
| Customer action | Approve once | None |

## Refunds

Refund an iDEAL payment from the order in [Orders](/sales/orders) with **Refund payment**. As with every refund on Kelviq, the transaction fee isn't returned and the tax part goes back to the customer.
